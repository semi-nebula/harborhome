About us

Built because student life is too scattered.

Most students live between bookmarks, notes, PDFs, Drive folders, mocks, formulas, chat links and random tabs. Harbor Home brings that mess into one calm dashboard.

It starts as a homepage, but grows into an exam-prep system: one place to start the day, track what matters and keep your brain clear.

Local-first by defaultUse it without an account. Your core dashboard lives in your browser unless you choose Google Backup.
Made for Indian examsTemplates for JEE, NEET, CETs, boards, UPSC, CLAT, CAT and more create a useful starting point instantly.
Designed to feel calmNot a loud productivity app. The goal is a quiet study table that helps you begin.

Privacy-first

Your dashboard belongs to you.

Harbor Home works fully in your browser. You can use it without an account. Google Backup is optional, and uploaded files are never sent to the cloud.

✓Use the dashboard signed out, with local browser storage.
✓Back up dashboard data to Google only if you choose to sign in.
✓PDFs, images and documents uploaded in Resources stay local-only.
Install it like an app.Add it to your home screen, open it quickly, and keep the app shell available offline.
